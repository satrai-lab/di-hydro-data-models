## Definition

An **AE PZT Sensor** is a piezoelectric transducer used for **Acoustic Emission (AE)**–based structural health and condition monitoring. Mounted directly on a structure, it converts high-frequency elastic waves—generated by crack initiation, friction, or mechanical stress releases—into voltage signals. These signals are digitized and analyzed to detect and locate defects, assess fatigue progression, and support predictive maintenance.
